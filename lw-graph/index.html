Two things the shape is doing. Output is bursty — 19 active days out of 117 — and it climbs after both programmes: 555 comment words across April and May, 1,917 across June and July.

The obvious causal reading is weaker than it looks. The 2,014-word peak is 57 per cent one artefact, an essay drafted over several weeks and merely published that Monday; counting comments alone, the fortnight after HAAISS runs 3.1× the fortnight before, and widening to three weeks drops that to 1.5×. The account is also only four months old, so the trend is confounded with simply getting started. Two events is not a sample you can fit a line through.

Comment and post word counts from the LessWrong GraphQL API, fetched 1 August 2026; weeks begin Monday. HAAISS 2026 ran 13–16 July in Prague. The GCP workshop is placed across June because I have confirmation that I attended but no record of which days, so its band marks the month rather than the event.
