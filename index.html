Hi, I’m Kurt Pieper! I mostly think about the creation of the great machine, and what ought to be done about it. You can find my writing on Lesswrong, or comb through my Recommendations, containing various interesting articles. Contact me via mail, or leave anonymous feedback.

Some questions I currently find interesting:

  • Can we constrain the behaviour of RL Models with the PSM, e.g. get them to stop doing stuff. If not, what else might have predictive power we can use constructively?
  • Does pushing down the reconstruction loss of NLA give us better insights into internal thoughts, and how would we even know?
  • Where do LLMs lie on the Spearman-Spectrum, i.e. how general are they, and does the information bottleneck of RLVR represent a natural Occam’s razor empirically, similar to humans1?
  1. Humans face the genomic bottleneck which means that each individual has to rederive all the knowledge about the world that their parents already had. If this genetic bottleneck hadn’t been so tight, then individual humans would have been significantly less capable of performing novel tasks.

    An agreement in the 2021 MIRI Conversations. Recently, we also learned a little selection pressure suffices for making humans a bunch smarter. ↩
